Fill out Workday and Greenhouse job applications in one click, using a profile you enter once. It stays on your computer unless you choose to sign in.

Applying to jobs means typing the same information over and over: your name, your address, your last three employers, your degree, your graduation date. Workday: Better Autofill enters all of it for you, so you can spend your time on the parts of an application that actually matter.


WHAT IT FILLS

Personal details
• First and last name, email, phone number
• LinkedIn or portfolio URL
• Street address, city, state, postal code, country

Work experience
• Company, job title, and location for each role
• Start and end dates, including "I currently work here"
• Role description

Education
• School, degree, and field of study
• Start and end dates
• Expected graduation month and year
• GPA

Skills and languages
• Comma-separated skill lists
• Primary language

Application questions
• Are you legally authorized to work in this country?
• Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?
• Are you 18 years of age or older?
• Have you previously worked for this company?
• How did you hear about us?

Voluntary self-identification
• Gender, race and ethnicity, veteran status, disability status
• Optional master toggle that selects the "decline to self-identify" or
  "prefer not to answer" option on every disclosure question at once

Resume
• Stores your resume file and attaches it to the application's upload field


SUPPORTED JOB BOARDS

Workday (enabled by default)
Works on any company careers site hosted on myworkdayjobs.com. Multi-page
applications are handled as you go: the extension watches for new form
sections and keeps filling as you click through each step.

Greenhouse (optional, off until you turn it on)
Supports both generations of Greenhouse boards - the classic boards.greenhouse.io
forms and the newer job-boards.greenhouse.io React application - including forms
that companies embed in their own careers pages.

Greenhouse support is off by default and requests no permissions at install
time. Turn it on under "Supported Portals" in the side panel and Chrome will
ask for access to Greenhouse domains at that moment. Switch the toggle back off
and access is revoked immediately.


PRIVACY

Without an account (the default), there is no sign-in and nothing is sent
anywhere:

• Your profile is stored locally using Chrome's storage API
• Nothing is uploaded, synced, or backed up
• Your data leaves the browser only when you click the autofill button, which
  types it into the job application in front of you - exactly as you would have
  typed it yourself

With an optional account:

• The profile, resume versions, application records and saved answers you
  choose to sync are stored with Amazon Web Services in the United States,
  encrypted, and visible only to you
• AI features send only what that feature needs (never your contact details)
  to Amazon Bedrock in the United States, which does not store it or train on it
• "Delete cloud account" removes your account and all of its data

Either way:

• No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs
• Your data is never sold
• Your self-identification answers (gender, race and ethnicity, veteran status,
  disability status) are optional, stay on your computer, and are never synced
  or uploaded. Leave them blank and those questions are left untouched.

The extension can only run on the job board domains listed above and on its
own service. It has no access to any other website you visit.

Delete local data at any time with the "Delete All Data" button in the side
panel. Uninstalling the extension also removes all locally stored data.

HOW TO USE

1. Click the extension icon to open the side panel. Unlike a popup, the panel
   stays open while you work through the form.
2. Fill in your profile and click "Save Profile". You only do this once.
3. Open a job application on a supported board.
4. Click "AUTOFILL ACTIVE PAGE".
5. Review what was filled, correct anything the form does differently, and
   submit.

Step 5 matters. Companies customise these forms heavily, and question wording
varies from posting to posting. The extension matches fields by their labels and
fills what it recognises. Always read the application over before you submit it.


BACKUP AND RESTORE

Export your profile to a JSON file and import it on another computer, or keep it
as a backup before clearing your browser data.


DESIGN NOTES

A few deliberate choices worth knowing about:

• When the extension is unsure, it leaves the field blank rather than guessing.
  A blank field costs you a few seconds; a wrong answer goes out on a real job
  application under your name.
• Fields that already contain a value are never overwritten, so anything you
  typed yourself or a resume parser filled in stays as you left it.
• Degree and GPA answers are mapped to whatever options the board actually
  offers - a "Bachelor of Science" becomes "Bachelors" or "Bachelors Degree"
  depending on the form. If a board offers no option matching your degree, the
  field is left for you.
• Greenhouse permissions are requested only if you ask for Greenhouse support,
  not at install time.


LIMITATIONS

• Only Workday and Greenhouse are supported. Other job boards are not.
• Heavily customised forms, or questions phrased in unusual ways, may not be
  recognised. The extension fills what it can and leaves the rest.
• Some employers use screening questions specific to one posting. Those are
  yours to answer.
• This is an autofill tool, not an auto-apply bot. It never submits an
  application for you. You review and submit every one yourself.
