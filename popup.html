                What is sent, and what never is

                After each autofill it sends only: which of the extension's own steps failed or were skipped and why (for example "Work Experience: did not change"), the form's internal ID and type for required fields left empty, the site's host name (like company.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com) and the extension version. Never sent: your answers or profile, question text, names, the job or page address, self-identification, or your account. Reports are not signed in and are kept for up to 12 months.
